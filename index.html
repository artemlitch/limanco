What We Do

Hardwood Expertise,
Every Detail Matters

From the grain of the wood to the finish on the floor — quality craftsmanship in every plank

Hardwood & Laminate Flooring

Expert installation of solid hardwood, engineered hardwood, and premium laminate flooring. We work with oak, maple, walnut, cherry, and exotic species on any subfloor — plywood, concrete, or radiant heat.

Hardwood Stair Refinishing

Complete staircase transformation with real hardwood — refinishing worn treads, rebuilding with solid wood or laminate, and crafting brand new hardwood staircases from scratch.

Interior Wood Doors

Professional installation of solid wood and wood-veneer interior doors, closet slide and bi-fold doors, plus precision fitting of door trims and hardware.

Wood Moldings & Trim

Hand-fitted installation of solid wood and MDF baseboards, casings, and crown moldings — the finishing touch that frames every room in your home.
